# Giving SoCal access to your Facebook and Instagram

So we can schedule your approved posts, your Facebook Page and Instagram
account get shared with our business account as a partner. You keep full
ownership and full control; partner access can be removed by you at any time.

You will need: admin access to your Facebook Page, an Instagram
professional account linked to that Page, and about ten minutes.

## 1. If you have not already: link Instagram to your Facebook Page

Instagram app -> Settings -> Business tools and controls -> Linked accounts ->
link your Facebook Page. (If your Instagram is a personal account, switch it
to a professional account first: Settings -> Account type.)

## 2. Add us as a partner

1. Go to business.facebook.com/settings (log in with the account that
   administers your Page).
2. In the left menu, choose **Partners** (under Users), then **Add** ->
   **Give a partner access to your assets**.
3. Enter our Business ID: **{{LINNFLUX_BUSINESS_ID}}**
4. Select your **Facebook Page** and your **Instagram account** as the assets
   to share.
5. For the access level, enable **Content** (creating and managing posts).
   Full control is not needed.
6. Confirm.

## 3. Tell us it is done

Reply to our email and we will confirm the connection from our side the same
day. Nothing gets posted at any point without your written approval; access
only lets the posts you have already approved go out on schedule.

To remove access later: Business settings -> Partners -> select Linnflux ->
remove. That is it.
